# Floodlight FC

A Godot 4.5 prototype with a main menu, AI opponent, team colors, sprinting, pause support, and stronger football gameplay polish.

## Features
- Main menu scene with Play / Controls / Quit
- AI opponent and local football prototype
- Team colors and stadium styling
- Match timer, scoreboard, and reset flow
- Sprinting, pause support, and refined gameplay feel
- Improved AI defending and stronger shot response

## Controls
- Player 1: Arrow keys + Tab jump
- AI opponent: auto-chases the ball and defends goal area
- Shift: sprint (player 1)
- L: kick (player 1)
- R: reset positions
- N: reset match score
- P: pause
- Esc: quit

## Notes
This remains a prototype foundation for an indie football game, not a full commercial sports title.
